# US-17: Course Detail Card & Prerequisite Chain

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-17` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Provide an inspectable Course Detail Card modal on `/planner` showing full course description, ECTS credits, difficulty rating, transitive upstream prerequisite chain, downstream unlocked courses, and season availability.

## Implementation

- Added [`CourseDetailDto`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/model/dto/CourseDetailDto.java) containing course description, ECTS credits, difficulty score and formatted label (`1/5 — Very Light` to `5/5 — Very Challenging`), direct prerequisites, transitive `prerequisiteChain`, downstream `unlocksCourseCodes`, and season label (`Fall`, `Spring`, `Fall & Spring`).
- Added `GET /planner/courses/{courseId}` in [`PlannerController`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerController.java) with cycle-safe recursive prerequisite chain traversal.
- Added `"📋 Details"` buttons (`.btn-course-details`) and the `#course-detail-modal` dialog in [planner.html](../src/main/resources/templates/planner.html), [planner.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/planner.js), and [planner.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/planner.css).

## Verification

- [PlannerControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerControllerTest.java) verifies `#course-detail-modal` rendering on `/planner` and validates full `CourseDetailDto` output for `CSS217` (including transitive chain `CSS105 -> INF108 -> CSS215`) and `CSS215` (downstream unlocks `CSS217`, `INF319`).
