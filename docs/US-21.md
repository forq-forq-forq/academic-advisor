# US-21: Season Availability Flagging (Fall-Only / Spring-Only)

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-21` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Flag courses offered only in Fall or Spring whenever they are placed in an incompatible semester on the 8-semester roadmap so students do not accidentally plan a Spring-only course for a Fall semester or vice versa.

## Implementation

- Enhanced [`RoadmapService`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapService.java) to evaluate Fall/Spring offering compatibility on non-completed courses (`odd semester = FALL`, `even semester = SPRING`), populate `seasonMismatch`, `seasonWarning`, and `seasonConflictCount`, and prefer season-compatible placements during `BALANCED` pacing.
- Added `#roadmap-season-conflicts` counter pill, `.badge-season` (`Fall Only` / `Spring Only`) tags, `.card-season-mismatch` amber border/highlight, and `.badge-season-conflict` warning banners in [planner.html](../src/main/resources/templates/planner.html), [planner.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/planner.js), and [planner.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/planner.css).

## Verification

- [RoadmapServiceTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapServiceTest.java) verifies flagging when moving `INF202` (Spring-only) into Fall Semester 5 and `INF381` (Fall-only) into Spring Semester 6, and clearing when moved back to a compatible term.
- [PlannerControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerControllerTest.java) verifies `#roadmap-season-conflicts`, `.badge-season` badges on `/planner`, and `seasonConflictCount` / `seasonMismatch` on `POST /planner/roadmap/move`.
