# US-26: Scholarship & Academic Probation Risk Warnings

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-26` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Alert students on `/gpa` whenever their simulated expected grades cause their projected cumulative GPA to drop below the state scholarship retention threshold (`< 3.00` or any failing grade `F`/`FX`/`AW`) or trigger academic probation standing (`< 2.00`).

## Implementation

- Extended [`GpaSimulationResultDto`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/model/dto/GpaSimulationResultDto.java) and [`GpaSimulationService`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/GpaSimulationService.java) with `belowScholarshipThreshold`, `hasFailingGrade`, `academicProbationRisk`, `riskLevel` (`SAFE`, `SCHOLARSHIP_WARNING`, `PROBATION_DANGER`), `scholarshipWarningMessage`, and `probationWarningMessage`.
- Added `#gpa-probation-warning`, `#gpa-scholarship-warning`, and `#gpa-safe-banner` alert cards with live updates in [gpa.html](../src/main/resources/templates/gpa.html), [gpa.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/gpa.js), and [gpa.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/gpa.css).

## Verification

- [GpaSimulationServiceTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/GpaSimulationServiceTest.java) validates failing-grade scholarship loss warnings (`F`/`FX`/`AW`), `< 3.00` scholarship threshold warnings, and `< 2.00` academic probation triggers.
- [GpaControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/GpaControllerTest.java) verifies warning card rendering on `GET /gpa` and risk warning JSON fields on `POST /gpa/simulate`.
