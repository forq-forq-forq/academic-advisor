# US-10: Recommended Next Action Hero Card

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-10` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Provide an adaptive, single-click **Recommended Next Action** hero card at the top of the student Dashboard so students always see their highest-priority next step based on active registration windows, semester cart state, and transcript completeness.

## Implementation

- Added [`RecommendedActionDto`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/model/dto/RecommendedActionDto.java) and [`RecommendedActionService`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RecommendedActionService.java) evaluating:
  1. Active/upcoming registration window + empty cart → `"Build your Fall 2026 plan"` → `/planner`.
  2. Non-empty semester cart → `"Finalize your semester cart (...)"` → `/planner`.
  3. Zero completed credits recorded → `"Record your completed coursework"` → `/profile`.
  4. Default balanced state → `"Explore courses for your next semester"` → `/planner`.
- Wired `recommendedAction` in [`DashboardController`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/DashboardController.java) and rendered `#recommended-next-action-card` alongside `#registration-alert-card` in [dashboard.html](../src/main/resources/templates/dashboard.html) and [dashboard.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/dashboard.css).

## Verification

- [RecommendedActionServiceTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RecommendedActionServiceTest.java) validates recommendation branching across registration window, cart items, and transcript state.
- [DashboardControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/DashboardControllerTest.java) confirms `#recommended-next-action-card` (`"Build your Fall 2026 plan"`, `"Open Planner"`) renders on `/dashboard`.
