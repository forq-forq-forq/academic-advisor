# US-19: Drag-and-Drop Roadmap Editing & Instant Validation

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-19` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Enable students to drag and drop courses across all 8 semesters (or use accessible move/remove controls) with instant prerequisite violation highlighting (red) and semester overload highlighting (yellow when > 30 ECTS).

## Implementation

- Added `moveCourseToSemester` and `removeCourseFromRoadmap` to [`RoadmapService`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapService.java), validating prerequisite ordering across semesters and flagging semesters exceeding `30` ECTS as overloaded.
- Added `POST /planner/roadmap/move` and `POST /planner/roadmap/remove` endpoints in [`PlannerController`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerController.java).
- Added HTML5 Drag-and-Drop handlers (`handleRoadmapDragStart`, `handleRoadmapDragOver`, `handleRoadmapDrop`), semester move dropdowns, remove buttons, `#roadmap-validation-bar`, `.card-prereq-violation` (red), and `.semester-overloaded` (yellow) in [planner.html](../src/main/resources/templates/planner.html), [planner.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/planner.js), and [planner.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/planner.css).

## Verification

- [RoadmapServiceTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapServiceTest.java) tests moving a course ahead of its prerequisite (`CSS217` before `CSS215`) and overloading a semester (> 30 ECTS).
- [PlannerControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerControllerTest.java) tests `POST /planner/roadmap/move` and `POST /planner/roadmap/remove` endpoints and HTML drag-and-drop attributes.
