# US-03: Onboarding & Career Aspirations

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-03` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Capture student career aspirations cleanly during registration onboarding, persist them across both `StudentAccount` and `Student`, display them on the post-registration onboarding summary, and feed richer career keyword matching into Semester Plan recommendations.

## Implementation

- Added `career_goal` column to `StudentAccount` ([StudentAccount.java](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/model/StudentAccount.java)) via additive migration [V5__add_account_career_goal.sql](../src/main/resources/db/migration/V5__add_account_career_goal.sql), and synchronized trimming/persistence in [`StudentRegistrationService.register`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/StudentRegistrationService.java).
- Added `#aspiration-presets` quick-select chips (`AI / Machine Learning Engineer`, `Backend Software Engineer`, `Data Scientist & Analytics`, `Cloud & DevOps Engineer`, `Cybersecurity Specialist`, `Product & Systems Analyst`) in [register.html](../src/main/resources/templates/register.html), styled in [auth.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/auth.css) and synced interactively in [register.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/register.js).
- Rendered `#onboarding-career-goal` banner on [registration-success.html](../src/main/resources/templates/registration-success.html) when `account.careerGoal` is provided.
- Updated `SemesterPlanService.matchesCareerGoal` ([SemesterPlanService.java](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/SemesterPlanService.java)) to filter common stopwords and map Cybersecurity, Data Analytics, Cloud/DevOps, Product/Systems, and Mobile/Frontend aspirations to related courses.

## Verification

- [CareerAspirationOnboardingTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/CareerAspirationOnboardingTest.java) tests preset chip rendering, persisted `careerGoal` on `Student` & `StudentAccount`, onboarding banner visibility, and AI plan career matching.
