# US-20: Pacing Strategy Roadmap Rearrangement

> **Status:** Implemented  
> **Story Reference:** `US-20` ([USER_STORY_MAP.md](USER_STORY_MAP.md))

## Goal

Allow students to choose between `"Balanced 4 years"` (`BALANCED`) and `"Fast-track: light 4th year for internship"` (`FAST_TRACK`) pacing strategies and automatically rearrange their multi-year roadmap while preserving prerequisite validity.

## Implementation

- Added `applyPacingStrategy(String studentId, String strategy)` to [`RoadmapService`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapService.java), locking completed/enrolled/past semesters and topologically sorting movable courses so `BALANCED` evens credits across future semesters while `FAST_TRACK` front-loads courses into Semesters 4–6 to free up Year 4 (Semesters 7–8) for internships.
- Added `POST /planner/roadmap/pacing` in [`PlannerController`](../src/main/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerController.java).
- Added `#roadmap-pacing-controls`, `#pacing-strategy-select`, `#btn-apply-pacing`, and `#roadmap-pacing-summary` in [planner.html](../src/main/resources/templates/planner.html), [planner.js](../src/main/resources/static/js/planner.js), and [planner.css](../src/main/resources/static/css/planner.css).

## Verification

- [RoadmapServiceTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/service/RoadmapServiceTest.java) verifies zero prerequisite conflicts for both strategies and confirms `FAST_TRACK` produces a lighter Year 4 credit load than `BALANCED`.
- [PlannerControllerTest.java](../src/test/java/kz/edu/sdu/advisor/controller/PlannerControllerTest.java) verifies pacing controls on `GET /planner` and `POST /planner/roadmap/pacing`.
