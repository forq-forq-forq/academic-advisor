Academic FAQ & Regulations

GPA & Grading

Cumulative GPA is calculated by dividing the total quality points earned by the total credit hours attempted across all completed semesters.

The minimum passing grade for core major courses is "C" (2.0), while general requirements require at least a "D" grade (1.0).

Registration

Registration dates depend on your current academic year and credit count. Check the official academic calendar for exact priority registration windows.

Retakes

You can retake a course up to 2 times. The highest grade achieved will replace the previous grade in your cumulative GPA calculation.

Summer School

Students can enroll in a maximum of 12 credit hours during the summer term.

Scholarships

State scholarship holders must maintain a GPA of at least 3.0 without any failing grades during the examination period.
